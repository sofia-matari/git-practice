I chose an article from MIT News
[New Method enables AI for safety-critical situations](https://news.mit.edu/2026/new-method-enables-ai-safety-critical-situations-0914)

### Why I Found It Interesting

I found this article interesting because it outlines a serious obstacle regarding AI development, which is how to make AI systems safe to use in scenarios where a potential mistake can have extremely harmful effects 

MIT researchers uncovered a method that would allow AI to follow strict guidelines. I find it very interesting how researchers are constantly innovating to find new ways to make AI safer and more reliable to use.

This article also emphasizes that AI development is not only about strengthening the accuracy and capabilities of the algorithms, but just as much, if not more so about safety and how these AI systems will perform in delicate environments when mistakes can be costly.
